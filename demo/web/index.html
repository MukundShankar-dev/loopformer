What you’re seeing

A prediction can return to the correct node after an earlier mistake. That is why final-answer correctness and the complete trajectory are scored separately.

Recorded examples are real, audited runs of the final checkpoint. New graphs require the live model service. The compact architecture view is explanatory, not an attention visualization.
